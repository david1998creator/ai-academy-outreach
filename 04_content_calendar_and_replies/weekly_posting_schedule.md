# 30-Day Multi-Channel Content & Outreach Schedule

*A structured weekly posting rhythm designed to maximize outreach velocity and create a predictable flow of admissions for Waynautic Academy.*

---

## 1. Prime Posting Times (IST - Indian Standard Time)

| Channel | Best Time Window | Content Type | Why |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **LinkedIn** | `08:30 AM – 10:15 AM` | Thought Leadership / Tips / Stories | Techies browse during morning transit & before standup |
| **LinkedIn** | `05:30 PM – 07:00 PM` | "Comment for Resource" Lead Magnets | End of workday casual browsing, high comment activity |
| **WhatsApp Groups** | `11:00 AM – 12:30 PM` | Short punchy drops + Flyer | Mid-day break, active chatting |
| **WhatsApp Broadcast** | `06:30 PM – 08:00 PM` | Webinar invites & Consultation links | Evening relaxation, higher attention span for career choices |
