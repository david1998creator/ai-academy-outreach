# Fast Comment Reply Playbook: LinkedIn & Social Media

*Rule of Thumb: The first 60 minutes after posting dictate 80% of LinkedIn's algorithmic reach. Respond to all comments within 15–30 minutes.*

---

## 1. Comment Reply Matrix

### Scenario A: Comment on Lead Magnet ("ROADMAP", "CHECKLIST", "INFO")
*Public Reply on LinkedIn:*
> "Sent straight to your DMs, [Name]! Check your message requests. Let me know which module you're diving into first! 🚀"

*Private LinkedIn DM Message:*
> "Hey [Name]! Here is your download link for the 2026 AI Engineering Roadmap: [Link].
> Quick question — are you currently a student preparing for placements, or working as a software developer? We're hosting a free 15-min AI career strategy call this week if you want a custom roadmap!"

---

### Scenario B: Thoughtful / Technical Comment
*Example: "Great point about hybrid search. Do you prefer Cohere Reranker or BGE?"*

*Public Reply:*
> "Spot on, [Name]! For lightweight open-source setups, BGE-Reranker-Large running locally via HuggingFace is fantastic for cost efficiency. For high-throughput enterprise SaaS, Cohere Reranker API gives unbeatable latency and multi-language support. What stack are you currently experimenting with?"

*Conversion Follow-Up (Later in DM):*
> "Hey [Name], loved your comment on the RAG post. If you're building production AI systems right now, you might enjoy our 4-Week Cohort where we implement these exact pipelines under 1-on-1 mentor guidance. Here's our full syllabus: https://academy.waynautic.com/"

---

### Scenario C: Price / Fee Inquiry on Comments
*Example: "What are the fees for this course?" or "Is this free?"*

*Public Reply:*
> "Hey [Name]! We have options for every stage: We offer a 100% Free 1-on-1 Consultation & Roadmap session, a ₹19 Live Masterclass, and our flagship 4-Week AI Intensive + 3-Month Industry Internship. 
> I just sent you the full pricing & syllabus breakdown directly in your DMs!"

---

### Scenario D: Skeptical Comment
*Example: "Anyone can learn this on YouTube for free. Why pay for a cohort?"*

*Public Reply:*
> "100% agree that information is free on YouTube, [Name]! 
> But what YouTube doesn't give you is: 
> 1) A dedicated 1-on-1 mentor doing line-by-line code reviews on your PRs,
> 2) Debugging tricky production errors in real-time,
> 3) A verifiable 3-Month Industry Internship on live projects, and
> 4) Dual certification to stand out to hiring managers.
> If someone is self-disciplined enough to build all that solo, more power to them! For those who want to shortcut the trial-and-error, structured mentorship changes everything."
