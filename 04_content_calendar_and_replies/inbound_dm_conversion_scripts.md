## 2. Handling Common Objections

### Objection 1: "I am a complete beginner / non-CS background. Can I join?"
```text
"Yes, absolutely! The first module starts right with Python fundamentals and Git version control before moving into LLM APIs and Vector Databases. 
Because every student gets a dedicated 1-on-1 mentor, your mentor moves at your personal pace to ensure you never get stuck."
```

### Objection 2: "Is the 3-Month Internship guaranteed?"
```text
"Yes! The 3-month industry internship is built directly into our flagship cohort. Once you complete the 4-week structured training and your portfolio projects, you transition directly into working on industry internship assignments at Waynautic, and you receive an official verifiable Internship Certificate."
```

### Objection 3: "I have college exams / office hours. Are the timings flexible?"
```text
"100% flexible. All video lecture topics and code labs are accessible on your student portal 24/7. Your live 1-on-1 mentorship sessions are scheduled directly between you and your mentor at times convenient to you (including evenings and weekends)."
```
