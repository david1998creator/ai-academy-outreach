## 1. Canva Step-by-Step Layout Guide

To assemble or customize flyers in Canva (Free or Pro):

1. **Canvas Setup**:
   * Create custom design: `1080 x 1350 px` (Standard high-res 4:5 Instagram & WhatsApp portrait).
   * Background color: `#F5EFEB` (Warm cream/linen).

2. **Top Header**:
   * Left: Waynautic Logo (or bold modern text `WAYNAUTIC ACADEMY` with small blue gradient geometric icon).
   * Right: Subhead `MARKET-READY TRAINING` (Uppercase, letter spacing 2.5px, Slate color `#6B7280`).

3. **Navy Hero Card**:
   * Add a rounded rectangle element (Border radius: `24px`).
   * Color: `#122443` (Deep midnight navy).
   * Title text: `AI skills are no longer optional. They're essential.` (Font: Plus Jakarta Sans / Inter Bold, White, with `essential.` in Amber Gold `#DDA15E`).
   * Body text: Regular 16px, off-white `#CBD5E1`.
   * Add 3 row containers with circular badge icons (Play button, Calendar, Shield).

4. **Middle Key Takeaway Section**:
   * Add vertical accent line in gold `#DDA15E` on the left.
   * Highlight bullet points: Bold font for headings, regular for descriptions.
   * Italic summary line at bottom.

5. **Bottom Navy CTA Bar**:
   * Dark navy rounded box (`#122443`).
   * Left side: `RESERVE YOUR SEAT` (Gold kicker), `Enroll in the training program` (White heading).
   * Center: High-contrast white QR Code with dark background (`Scan to Register`).
   * Right: Solid Amber Gold button card (`#DDA15E`) with dark black/navy text:
     * `CONTACT`
     * `Pramod Gogadare`
     * `9158998226`

## 3. Quick Export & File Size Best Practices for WhatsApp
* **Format**: Export as `.PNG` or high-quality `.JPG` (under 2MB for fast loading on mobile networks).
* **Color Space**: sRGB (ensures colors don't wash out on Android and iPhone screens).
* **Text Legibility Check**: Ensure all headlines are easily readable when viewed on a 6-inch phone screen without zooming in.
