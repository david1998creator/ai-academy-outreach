# WhatsApp Follow-Up Drip Sequences

*Use these structured follow-ups when a prospective student reaches out via WhatsApp or attends a webinar but hasn't finalized their enrollment yet.*

---

## Follow-Up 1: The Value Drop (24 Hours After Initial Inquiry)
*Goal: Provide instant free value, showing that you are invested in their success.*

```text
Hi [First Name]! Pramod here from Waynautic Academy. 

Hope you're having a productive week! 

I wanted to share our quick-reference PDF cheatsheet: *"Top 5 Production RAG Architecture Patterns for 2026"* [Attach PDF / Link].

Were you able to check out the curriculum on our portal (https://academy.waynautic.com/about)? Let me know if you have any questions about how the 1-on-1 mentorship works!
```

---

## Follow-Up 2: The Social Proof & Internship Spotlight (Day 3)
*Goal: Overcome hesitation by demonstrating real outcomes and the internship guarantee.*

```text
Hey [First Name]! 👋

A lot of students ask us: *"Will 4 weeks really be enough to build production AI skills?"*

The answer is: Yes, because every student gets a *dedicated 1-on-1 mentor* who reviews your code directly, followed by our *3-Month Industry Internship*. You leave with dual certifications and 4–5 working agentic apps on your GitHub.

Our next cohort has only 6 mentor slots remaining to ensure personalized attention.

Would you like to hop on a quick 5-minute call today to see if this matches your career goals?
```

---

## Follow-Up 3: Urgency & Final Cohort Seat Closing (Day 5)
*Goal: Create real scarcity and close the loop.*

```text
Hi [First Name], quick update! ⏰

We are finalizing the batch roster for this month's AI Cohort today so our mentors can prep personalized roadmaps for each student.

If you are still planning to join this batch and lock in the 3-Month Industry Internship + Dual Certification, let me know within the next few hours so I can reserve your seat!

Reply *YES* if you want me to hold your spot, or let me know if next month suits you better.
```
